Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Telemaco is a personal-use software assistant built and operated by, and exclusively for, Andrea Cipriani. There is exactly one user. This policy describes how data accessed through connected third-party services (such as WHOOP or Google) is handled.

What data is accessed

Depending on which third-party services Andrea has connected, Telemaco may access data such as: fitness and health metrics (e.g. recovery, strain, sleep, and workout data from WHOOP), or email, calendar, and file data (e.g. from Google). Access is always scoped to what a given feature needs, and always granted explicitly through that provider's own OAuth consent screen — Telemaco never receives more than what Andrea approves there.

How data is stored and used

Data is stored on hardware Andrea owns and controls, or in private, access-restricted repositories he owns. It is used only to fulfill Andrea's own requests to his assistant — for example, logging a workout, drafting an email, or summarizing recent activity.

Telemaco is built using Anthropic's Claude as its underlying AI model. In the course of fulfilling a request, relevant data may be processed by Claude to generate a response; this is subject to Anthropic's own privacy practices.

What is never done with this data

Data is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never shared with third parties beyond what's described above. There is no mailing list, no analytics tracking, and no other user this data could be shared with even in principle.

Revoking access / deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected service's own account or security settings (e.g. WHOOP's or Google's app permissions page). To request deletion of any data Telemaco has stored, contact the address below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Telemaco connects to new services. The date above reflects the most recent change.
