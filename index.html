Telemaco

A personal AI assistant, operated by Andrea Cipriani.

Telemaco is a single-user, personal-use software assistant. It runs on hardware Andrea Cipriani owns and controls, and it exists solely to automate tasks on his behalf — email, calendar, notes, and personal data tracking (such as fitness and health metrics). It is not a public product, and it does not accept sign-ups from other users.

This site exists mainly to host the documentation required by third-party services Telemaco connects to on Andrea's behalf.
